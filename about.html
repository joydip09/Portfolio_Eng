I'm Joy — a builder obsessed with hardware, code, and the space where they meet. I work on IoT devices, robots, embedded systems, and electronics experiments. This portfolio is my lab notebook — every success, every failure, documented.

Philosophy

How I Think About Building

"Process over perfection. Every failure is a datapoint. The goal is to understand deeply, not just to make things work — but to know why they work."

🔬
Experiment First

Build something rough, test it, break it, understand it. Clean up later.

📓
Document Everything

Failures are just as valuable as successes. Write it all down.

🔁
Iterate Relentlessly

Version 1 is never the answer. Version 5 might be.
